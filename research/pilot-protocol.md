# Pilot and buyer-validation protocol

Hypothesis: a written channel surfaces questions that otherwise remain unasked.
Recruit one consenting class and lecturer; disclose teacher-visible identities before use.
Record session duration, questions submitted/answered, response delay and an optional anonymous
usefulness rating. Do not infer learning gains from question counts alone. Avoid grades and
identifiable responses in public reports. Define retention/deletion before collection.
Compare equivalent sessions if feasible and record contextual differences. Give students an
oral participation alternative. The current repository provides a working MVP, not invented pilot results.

Buyer interview: who owns teaching tools, current alternatives, procurement process, acceptable
privacy model, willingness to trial, budget constraints and reasons not to buy. Record actual
responses before replacing scenario prices with validated commercial numbers.
