# Pitch outline

1. Problem: useful questions remain unasked for different reasons.
2. Users and buyer: students/lecturers use it; institutions may buy it.
3. Research: three exploratory interviews motivate privacy, answer quality and moderation.
4. Product: demonstrate a named submission, public anonymous projection and teacher answer.
5. Alternatives: LMS, polling tools, chat and oral questions; test differentiation rather than assert monopoly.
6. Business model: hypothetical subscription and support; show sensitivity in finance.py.
7. Execution: pilot one class, evaluate response process, then test institutional demand.
8. Request: permission for a small consent-based pilot and feedback, not an invented funding success.
